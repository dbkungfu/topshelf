Top Shelf

Rank your board game collection v1.6.1

1Add games

Drop box art onto the page, paste it, or click Add images. Save covers from each game's BoardGameGeek page, and name the files after the games. Or just click Try with sample games.

2Rank them

Drag a game into a tier, or click it and pick one. Hover a game and press 1–6 to rank it fast.

3Edit tiers

Click a tier's letter to rename it. Use ⋮ on a row for colour, order, clear or delete. × on a game sends it back to Unranked.

4Lists

Click the list title to switch, add or duplicate lists, or use the bin to delete one. ⋯ › Reset list clears the current list.

5Save and share

Pick a folder in Chrome or Edge and lists save automatically. Export PNG saves a picture; ⋯ › Back up all lists saves a copy.

6Toolbar

Tile size, game names, movie camera (Cinematic effect), eye (hover preview), palette (themes).
